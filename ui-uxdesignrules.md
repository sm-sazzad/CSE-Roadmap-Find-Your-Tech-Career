## 🧠 1. THE AUTONOMOUS ART DIRECTOR PROTOCOL (WHEN USER ONLY GIVES AN IDEA)

When the user says: *"I have an idea: [X], make it premium"*, you must NOT ask them for design preferences or fall back to standard templates. Follow this 4-step autonomous design synthesis:

### Step 1: Deduce the Soul & Emotional Vibe
Analyze the core domain of the idea and assign its emotional atmosphere:
* **Speed / Code / Dev Tools:** High-density, laser-focused, razor-sharp, monochromatic with vibrant telemetry accent.
* **Finance / Wealth / Crypto:** Institutional trust, obsidian dark or marble off-white, precision monospace metrics, gold or cobalt accents.
* **Health / Wellness / Life:** Serene, organic breathing room, soft sand/sage tones, fluid curves.
* **Creative / Fashion / Lifestyle:** High-fashion editorial, oversized serif typography, stark contrast, asymmetrical art gallery layout.

### Step 2: Invent the "Signature Hero Toy" (Interactive Metaphor)
Never create a static hero section. You must autonomously invent **one functional, interactive element** that lets the user *experience* the product idea:
* *Example for a gym/fitness idea:* An interactive "Workout Intensity Dial" that live-updates caloric burn and animates muscle heartbeats.
* *Example for an invoice/business idea:* A live interactive receipt/calculator where typing numbers live-renders a 3D tilting holographic invoice.
* *Example for a real-estate idea:* An interactive 3D floorplan perspective card with day/night light mode toggle.

### Step 3: Auto-Assign Distinctive Typography & Palette
Match the project to the Typography & Palette Matrices below. Never use Inter/Roboto for headlines; never use the cliché purple/pink gradient.

### Step 4: Over-Deliver on Physicality & Delight
Always include at least 2 layers of motion physics:
1. **Interactive Canvas / Particle Substrate:** Mouse-attracted or repelled particle network, reactive sound synthesis, or ambient spotlight.
2. **3D Tilt or Gyroscope Perspective:** Cards that physically tilt based on cursor angle with specular light reflections.

## 2. 🚫 THE "ANTI-AI" MANIFESTO (STRICT PROHIBITIONS)

Traditional AI web generation produces repetitive "AI slop" because it regresses to the statistical average of internet templates. You are strictly forbidden from the following:

| ❌ Prohibited AI-Slop Pattern | ✅ The Pro-Max Replacement |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Generic Purple/Pink Gradient** (`indigo-500` via `purple-500` to `pink-500`) | Distinctive domain-driven palettes (Matte Obsidian, Slate Brass, Swiss Monochrome, Cyber Lime). |
| **Inter / Roboto Everywhere** | Distinctive typography pairings: *Clash Display, Syne, Space Grotesk, Instrument Serif, Plus Jakarta Sans*. |
| **3 Equal Cards in a Row** | Asymmetrical **Bento Grids**, staggered cards, variable aspect ratios (2x1, 1x2, 2x2 spans). |
| **Emojis as Feature Icons** (🚀, 💡, ⚡) | Clean, precise SVG icons from `lucide-react`, `heroicons`, or inline SVGs. |
| **Flat Digital Look (No Texture)** | Subtle SVG noise grain overlays, ambient blurred glow blobs (`blur-3xl opacity-15`), soft inner borders. |
| **Jarring 0ms Transitions** | Smooth physics: `transition-all duration-300 ease-out`, micro-hover lifts (`hover:-translate-y-1`). |
| **Low Contrast Gray-on-White** | Strict WCAG AA compliance (minimum 4.5:1 text-to-background contrast ratio). |

## 3. 🎨 DOMAIN-SPECIFIC COLOR & STYLE MATRICES

Map the user's product to one of these production-tested design tokens:

### A. High-Tech AI & Developer Platforms (Dark Luxury & Obsidian)
* **Background:** Deep Matte Obsidian (`#09090b` or `#050507`)
* **Surfaces & Cards:** Tinted Zinc (`#111115`) with `border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`
* **Primary Accent:** Electric Emerald (`#00ff88`), Laser Amber (`#f59e0b`), or Cyber Cyan (`#00f0ff`)
* **Typography:** Headings: `Space Grotesk` or `Syne` | Body: `Plus Jakarta Sans` | Mono: `JetBrains Mono`

### B. FinTech, Banking & Enterprise Security (Institutional Trust)
* **Background:** Deep Navy Midnight (`#030712`) or Crisp Off-White (`#f8fafc`)
* **Surfaces:** Dark Sapphire Glass (`#0b1528` with `border-blue-500/20`) or Pure White with subtle shadow
* **Accents:** Electric Cobalt (`#2563eb`), Precision Cyan (`#06b6d4`), Muted Gold (`#d97706`)

### C. Creative Agencies, Studios & Portfolios (Swiss Typography & Architectural Minimal)
* **Background:** Warm Alabaster (`#fbfbfa`) or Deep Ink (`#111111`)
* **Surfaces:** Clean stark contrast, visible thin architectural grid lines (`border-neutral-200` or `border-neutral-800`)
* **Accents:** International Klein Blue (`#002fa7`) or Vermillion Red (`#e63946`)
* **Typography:** Headings: `Syne` (Bold/ExtraBold) | Body: `Plus Jakarta Sans`

### D. Luxury Lifestyle, Real Estate & Wellness (Warm Editorial)
* **Background:** Warm Bone Ivory (`#f7f5f0` or dark mode `#141312`)
* **Surfaces:** Warm Sandstone (`#ece7de` or `#1e1c1a`) with fine brass borders (`#c5a880`)
* **Accents:** Champagne Brass (`#c5a880`), Muted Olive (`#556b2f`), Deep Espresso (`#201c18`)
* **Typography:** Elegant editorial serif headers (`Instrument Serif`) with tight tracking and italic highlights

## 5. 🍱 COMPONENT CRAFTSMANSHIP & DELIGHT CHECKLIST

Every website must include these craftsmanship details:
1. **Interactive Hero Toy:** A dedicated widget or sandbox reflecting the product mechanics.
2. **3D Card Micro-Physics:** Gyroscopic tilt on hover with dynamic specular lighting.
3. **Ambient Light / Glow Effects:** Subtle blurred ambient light blobs (`blur-3xl opacity-15`).
4. **Noise Texture (Anti-Flatness):** An SVG noise filter or subtle grain overlay.
5. **Real SVG Icons:** Pure Lucide/Heroicons SVG paths (never emojis as icons).
